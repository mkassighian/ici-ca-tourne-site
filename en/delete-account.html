Delete your Ici ça tourne account

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. In the app

While signed in, go to Profile → Delete my account, then tap Delete. Deletion is immediate and permanent.

2. Without the app

Write to mikael.kassighian@gmail.com from your account's email address, with the subject “Account deletion”. Your request is handled within 30 days.

3. What is erased

  • Your account: email address, password or Google link.
  • Your username.
  • Your “I see it too” confirmations.
  • The abuse reports you made.
  • Your production search counter.

If you signed in with Google, you can also remove Ici ça tourne's access in your Google account settings.

4. What is kept

Your shoot reports stay visible, without an author name (“Reported by a deleted account”). To erase them too, delete them one by one in the app (shoot page → Delete my report) before deleting your account, or ask by email.

If your account was suspended, the SHA-256 fingerprint of your email address is kept to prevent signing up again (see the privacy policy).

5. Statistics

Statistics are not linked to your account. Turning off the Statistics switch in Profile stops sending them and erases the installation identifier from your phone. Statistics already sent are erased after 2 months (see Statistics).
