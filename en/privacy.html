Privacy policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This page explains what data the Ici ça tourne app processes, why, with whom, for how long, and how to exercise your rights.

1. Controller

Mikael Kassighian, Gimel (VD), Switzerland.
Contact: mikael.kassighian@gmail.com

2. Data processed to provide the service

  • Your email address and your encrypted password; or, if you sign in with Google, your Google name and email address (and profile picture, if Google provides it).
  • Your username.
  • Your shoot reports: location, address, production, production type, description, celebrities, date.
  • Your “I see it too” confirmations.
  • Your abuse reports: reason and comment.
  • A temporary counter of your production searches, which limits how many you can make per minute.
  • The hosting provider's technical logs (for example the IP address and time of requests).

Your phone's location is used to center the map and to place a report. It is only stored when you publish a report, and never in the background.

3. What is public

Shoot reports are visible to everyone, along with their author's username. The number of confirmations on a shoot is public, but not who confirmed: your confirmations are visible only to you.

4. Legal basis

  • The service (account, reports, confirmations, moderation): performance of the contract, that is, the terms of use.
  • Statistics: your consent.
  • The email fingerprint of suspended accounts (see Retention): our legitimate interest in protecting the community.

5. Statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

  • They are only turned on if you agree when you first open the app.
  • Data sent: an installation identifier, screens viewed, actions (reporting, confirming, opening a page…), device model, system version and approximate country.
  • Never sent: your account, username, email, location, or anything you type. No advertising, no advertising identifier.
  • Retention: 2 months.
  • You can withdraw your consent at any time in Profile → Statistics. Sending stops and the installation identifier is erased from your phone.

6. Service providers

  • Supabase: hosting and database, in the European Union (Ireland).
  • MapTiler: base map and address search. It receives your IP address, the area shown on screen, the location of a report (to find its address) and the text of an address search.
  • Google: Google sign-in and Firebase Analytics.
  • Brevo: sending emails (account emails, moderation alerts).
  • TMDB: posters and production search. It receives your IP address when posters load.

Some providers are located outside Switzerland and the European Union, notably in the United States. Depending on the provider, these transfers are covered by the Data Privacy Framework (EU–US and Swiss–US) or by standard contractual clauses.

7. Outbound links

IMDb, TMDB, Google Maps and Apple Maps open outside the app and follow their own privacy rules.

8. Retention

  • Account and username: until the account is deleted.
  • Shoot reports: kept after the account is deleted, but anonymized (shown as “Reported by a deleted account”). You can delete them yourself beforehand.
  • Confirmations: deleted with the account.
  • Abuse reports you made: deleted with the account.
  • Production search counter: only the last minute's searches count; older ones are erased as searches go on, and all of yours with the account.
  • Suspended accounts: the SHA-256 fingerprint of the email address is kept, even after the account is deleted, to prevent signing up again (legitimate interest).
  • Technical logs: a short period set by the hosting provider.
  • Statistics: 2 months.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access your data, have it corrected or erased, restrict its processing, object to its processing, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw your consent. Write to mikael.kassighian@gmail.com; you will get an answer within 30 days.

You can also lodge a complaint with the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC, Switzerland) or with the authority of your country in the European Union (in France, the CNIL).

10. Minimum age

You must be 16 or older to use Ici ça tourne.

11. Changes

The date at the top of this page shows the version in force. Any significant change will be announced in the app.
